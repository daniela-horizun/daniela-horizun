**Co-founder & COO at [Horizun Group](https://www.horizunaec.com)** — Digital Engineering & Construction.

I lead operations, administration and business development at Horizun Group, a digital engineering and construction company specialized in **BIM, Power BI and Artificial Intelligence** for the AEC industry. We help contractors, developers and design firms build with data across Colombia, Mexico and the United States.

## What we build

- **BuildMotion 4D** — a custom Power BI visual that runs real 4D construction simulations directly inside the dashboard, connected to BIM models, schedule data and KPIs. No external player, no disconnected workflows.
- **Power BIM** — BIM analytics inside Power BI: models, schedules, quantities and costs in a single source of truth.
- **Horizun Hub** — training and community platform for AEC professionals working with BIM, Power BI and AI: [horizunhub.com](https://horizunhub.com)
- **Revit automation** — pyRevit tool suites, Revit API add-ins and QA/QC workflows for production BIM teams.
